# Pricing update — October 2, 2026

All amounts are CAD, before applicable taxes. Ten active clients and unlimited
seats are included. Existing visit-based active-client counting is unchanged.

| Offer | Base | Additional client per monthly usage period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founding, first three customers | $200/month | $5 |
| Standard monthly | $350/month | $10 |
| Standard annual | $3,360/year prepaid ($280/month equivalent) | $10 |

Annual savings are 20% of the base only. Existing annual usage collection timing
is unchanged: calculate monthly, accumulate, collect at year-end after the
correction window. Cancelling stops renewal; access lasts through prepaid
coverage and the prepaid annual base is non-refundable.

Founding rates remain protected for twelve paid months, followed by Standard
rates with at least thirty days' notice. One thirty-minute feedback session each
month remains part of the offer. Testimonial and logo requests follow experienced
value and require separate approval; they are not conditions of access.

## Agreement handling

New purchases use Standard v3 or Founding v2. Prior versions and historical period
snapshots retain their original rates. Future founding conversion notices take
the current Standard terms; notices already issued retain their recorded terms.

The owner requested existing Standard customers move after notice. The live
Stripe subscription inventory was empty on October 2, so there is no live
subscriber migration or notice to send for this rollout. Existing sandbox test
subscriptions remain intact. Recheck the live inventory immediately before
release. If customers have subscribed in the meantime, record and deliver notice
before scheduling their transition; do not rewrite agreements or accrued usage
in place. No generic automatic Standard migration job is introduced here.
